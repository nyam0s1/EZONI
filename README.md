# 🌾 Ezoni Farm ERP

A Digital Operating System for Modern Agriculture

EZONI Farm is a Farm Management System designed to bring the daily operations of livestock and crop farming into one place. It replaces manual record-keeping with a central dashboard for animals, farm supplies, produce, sales, and expenses.

🌐 [Open EZONI Farm](https://ezoni-farm.vercel.app/)

### Repository branches

* `main`: Stable web application.
* `web`: Web application source, kept aligned with `main`.
* `android`: Android application source, with the mobile app currently being tested.

🚀 Key Features

🐄 Smart Livestock Tracking

* Animal Records: Keeps names, species, breeds, dates of birth, and mother records together.
* Batch & Tagging: Supports individual tagging (e.g., COW-001) or batch management for flocks (e.g., FLOCK-A).
* Status History: Tracks animals as active, sick or isolated, deceased, or sold, with a history of changes.

🌱 Stock & Produce Management

* Farm Supplies: Records feed, medicine, and other inputs as they are stocked and used.
* Harvests & Sales: Tracks produce quantities, harvests, customer sales, and revenue.
* Low-Stock Alerts: Highlights supplies and produce that reach their chosen minimum levels.

💰 Financial Engine

* Income & Expenses: Compares sales revenue with recorded restocking costs to show profit or loss for a selected period.
* Visual Reports: Shows income trends over custom date ranges.
* Transaction History: Keeps sales and supply costs available for review.

🔐 Access & Accountability

* Admin & User Access: Separate roles for managing the system and handling day-to-day farm work.
* Activity History: Records changes to animals, stock, produce, and user accounts, including who made them and when.
* Account Management: Admins can approve new users and manage access.

📱 Web & Android

* Mobile-Friendly Design: The familiar dark interface adapts to computers and smartphones.
* Android App: Currently being tested, bringing the same live farm system to Android.
* Live Records: An internet connection is needed to sign in and work with farm records. The Android offline preview shows layouts only.
